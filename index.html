About

I’m a Quality Engineering leader and automation architect focused on building scalable, production-grade test systems using Playwright, TypeScript, and modern CI/CD platforms. I design architectures that work across UI, API, and data layers and help teams move from fragile scripts to resilient, system-level validation.

My work spans framework design, test orchestration in distributed systems, in-sprint automation, and enterprise-scale adoption of modern QE practices. I enjoy solving hard problems around reliability, signal quality, and maintainability at scale.

A major part of my work today is integrating AI into the end-to-end testing lifecycle. I use large language models to accelerate test design, improve selector resilience, generate scenarios, and assist engineers during framework usage. This isn’t experimental AI. It is embedded directly into real automation frameworks and daily workflows.

My goal is simple: make quality a system property, not a hero effort. AI is a force multiplier in that journey.

AI in Testing

  • LLM-assisted test generation from requirements, stories, and UI state
  • Natural-language test intent mapped to executable Playwright code
  • AI-driven selector analysis and self-healing strategies
  • Agentic flows that explore applications and reason about behavior
  • Framework-level AI hooks for validation, diagnostics, and reporting
  • Semantic test impact analysis and intelligent prioritization

These capabilities are not standalone tools. They are part of the automation framework itself, allowing teams to create, evolve, and maintain tests faster while improving signal quality. The result is fewer brittle scripts, higher coverage, and a testing process that scales with product complexity.
