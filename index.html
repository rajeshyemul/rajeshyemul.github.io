About

I lead enterprise Quality Engineering modernization initiatives that transform automation from a support function into a scalable delivery enabler. With 20+ years in technology and 15+ years in automation strategy, my focus is on designing systems that improve release velocity, reduce operational cost, and strengthen engineering confidence at scale.

My work spans multi-program portfolios, framework standardization across teams, CI/CD-aligned automation ecosystems, and governance models that embed quality directly into delivery workflows. I design automation architectures that scale sustainably — not just technically, but organizationally.

A key pillar of my work today is AI-enabled Quality Engineering. I integrate AI as an optimization layer within automation systems — enhancing test generation, intelligent impact analysis, selector resilience, and diagnostic insight — while maintaining architectural discipline.

I believe sustainable quality comes from system design, architectural clarity, and strategic alignment — not heroics.

AI in Testing

The result is fewer brittle scripts, higher coverage, and a testing process that scales with product complexity.
